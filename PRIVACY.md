# Corner Mart — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-23

Corner Mart is an arcade-idle supermarket game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what
data the app handles and why.

## Summary

- We (the developer) do not collect, store or receive any personal data. There are no accounts,
  and we run no analytics of our own.
- Your game progress is saved only on your device.
- The app shows **optional rewarded video ads**. You choose to watch one to get an in-game bonus.
  These ads are served by **Unity LevelPlay** (formerly ironSource) and **Unity Ads**, both from
  Unity Technologies. Their software in the app collects some data, described below, to deliver
  and measure those ads.
- The game is playable without ever watching an ad. Rewards never depend on whether you allow
  tracking.

## Data collected by the advertising partners

When the app starts and when an ad is loaded or shown, the Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads SDKs may
collect:

| Data | Examples | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Device identifiers | Identifier for Vendors (IDFV); Advertising Identifier (IDFA) **only if you allow tracking** | Ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention; with your permission, personalised ads |
| Coarse location | Approximate location derived from your IP address (country / region) | Serving ads suited to your region, fraud prevention |
| Advertising data | Ads you were shown, ad views, clicks and completions | Ad delivery, measurement and reporting |
| Product interaction | App launches and in-app ad interactions | Ad delivery and measurement |
| Performance and diagnostics | Device model, OS version, app version, connection type, load times, SDK errors and crashes | Keeping ads working, troubleshooting |

This data is processed by Unity Technologies and, through them, by their advertising partners. It
is not sent to us, and we cannot link it to you. The advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used to
track you across apps and websites owned by other companies **only if you allow it** in the
system prompt described below.

## App Tracking Transparency

On first launch the app shows Apple's "Allow Corner Mart to track your activity across other
companies' apps and websites?" prompt. If you tap **Ask App Not to Track**, the IDFA is not shared
and you still receive (non-personalised) ads and every reward.

You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking** (turn off
"Allow Apps to Request to Track" or the switch for Corner Mart). You can also reset your
advertising identifier in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising**.

## Your choices and rights

- **Don't watch ads.** Ads play only when you tap a "Watch ad" or "Boost" button.
- **Limit tracking** with the iOS settings above.
- **Access or delete data held by Unity.** Because we hold no data about you, requests about ad
  data go to Unity: use Unity's privacy request form linked from the policy below, or email
  privacy@unity3d.com.
- **Delete your game progress** by deleting the app.

## Children

Corner Mart is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of the App Store Kids category.
The ads shown are filtered to the app's age rating.
